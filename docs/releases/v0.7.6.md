original map space preserved on compact panels.

## n1rwj-rbn

- Adds **Allow merging of RBN spots** in **Settings → RBN**, on by default.
  Nearby spots can merge and the RBN logging/activity control stays hidden.
  Turn it off when hunting individual stations, for example during CWT, to
  keep spots separately selectable and enable RBN references and the control.
- The merging choice survives restarts and applies on the next Spots refresh,
  including cached reports. Reset all spot settings restores merging.
  Existing contacts retain their saved RBN references.
- Adds a view-cycle button beside refresh and info in **My Signal**:
  **Map and receivers → Map → Receivers**. Its icon follows the current view.
  It preserves the original map space, including on compact panels.
- In-panel view choices belong to each placement and survive refreshes and
  unrelated settings changes. Changing the saved **View** default applies
  immediately; switching operations or restarting returns to that default.
  Band filters and sort choices remain independent.
