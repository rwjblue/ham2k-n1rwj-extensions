# v0.18.0 — NCCC Sprint CW and Zombie bonus stations

Adds an independent NCCC Sprint CW extension with serial/name/QTH logging,
per-band multipliers, weekly sessions and exports. Also identifies the 16
announced 2026 Zombie Shuffle bonus stations. These are draft notes for the
next release; publication has not occurred.

## n1rwj-mst

## n1rwj-ns

- New NCCC Sprint CW activity with Friday 0230–0300 UTC sessions, native sent
  serials, received serial/name/QTH fields and QRP/low-power setup.
- Scores eligible contacts once per band with US states, Canadian provinces
  and NS-specific country multipliers counted again on each band. Includes
  per-band QSO/multiplier summaries and checkpoint-safe duplicate detection.
- Adds native cached N1MM `SPRINTNS` name/QTH prefills alongside local history
  and host lookups, preserving corrections and clearing. Received serials
  are never history suggestions. NS now requires extension API 11.
- Adds CQ/S&P/Unknown entry and an optional NS Sprint guide panel. QSY and
  return-to-frequency reminders survive score checkpoints; frequency-only
  checks remain advisory. Every QSY reminder leaves scores and saving unchanged.
- Adds ADIF/Cabrillo exports and operating guidance for QSY, the response
  cutoff, weekly variations and prohibited Skimmer/packet assistance.
- [Operator guide](https://github.com/rwjblue/ham2k-n1rwj-extensions/blob/main/extensions/contests/n1rwj-ns/README.md).

## n1rwj-zombie

- Identifies the 16 bonus stations in NA5N's October 29 QRP-L announcement
  in 2026 lookup/spot notes and lists them in the offline event overview.
  `N7CQR` and `K7TQ` are separate stations. Announced assignments stay separate
  from copied exchanges and history hints; scoring still uses the number
  actually received, and operator corrections and clearing are preserved.
- Prefills `4321` for announced 2026 bonus calls ahead of older personal-number
  history. Entered numbers and deliberate clearing take precedence; confirm
  the complete exchange on air.
