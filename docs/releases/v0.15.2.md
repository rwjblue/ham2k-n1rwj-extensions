## n1rwj-skcc

- Preserve the compact logging name line when SKCC membership is found. Member
  number, suffix, status and roster hints appear as additional **Info** notes
  and in the companion panel, rather than a compact note that hides the name.
- Let QRZ, other callbooks and Call History supply station identity first.
  The offline roster name is a final fallback, and roster SPC remains a labeled
  hint. Existing names, locations, photos, call notes and explicit exchange
  corrections retain their normal behavior.
- The separate SKCC exchange controls continue to suggest roster name, SPC and
  member number for confirmation on air. No QRZ account or additional network
  request is needed for membership enrichment.
