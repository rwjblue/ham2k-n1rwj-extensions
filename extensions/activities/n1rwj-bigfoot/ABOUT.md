BIGFOOT Hunt covers the Radio Club of Tacoma’s October 13–21, 2026 special event.
The challenge was to work the event calls and spell **B I G F O O T**. The second
**W7O** contact had to be on a different band, mode, or UTC date. **W4S** was the
Skunk Ape bonus.

Add **BIGFOOT Hunt 2026** to the operation with your event contacts and Ham2K will
show you which letters you found in CW, Phone, Digital, and Mixed. The optional
**BIGFOOT Hunt** panel puts those letters in an animated forest where Bigfoot waves
when you log a new one. During the event, selecting a spot in the forest could
tune a connected radio.

Use the **BIGFOOT worksheet (CSV)** to review your 2026 contacts before filling
out the club’s certificate form. [Radio Club of Tacoma certificate instructions](https://w7dk.org/?catid=185&id=323:bigfoot-qsl-certificates&view=article)
