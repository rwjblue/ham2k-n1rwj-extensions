When you call CQ on FT8, it’s handy to see where your signal is getting
decoded. Add **PSK Reporter** to your operation’s layout and choose **Who hears
me** for a map and a sortable list of reception reports. The panel follows the
operation’s callsign and location by default.

Switch to **Who I hear** to see what your own receiving software reports.
Enable PSK Reporter uploads in that software and enter its exact receiver ID in
the panel settings if it differs from your operation’s callsign. Use **Band**
and **Window** to narrow either view.

The panel uses the internet to get live reports through M0LTE’s feed and look
up recent history when you open it. [PSK Reporter](https://pskreporter.info/) · [Live feed](https://www.mqtt.pskreporter.info/)
