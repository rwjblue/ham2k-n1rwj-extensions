Add **Contest Calendar** to your **Home** layout to see what’s running now and
what’s coming up over the next two weeks. Switch between UTC and local time,
filter by mode, and save the contests you’re thinking of working. Open a
contest to see its schedule and follow the sponsor’s rules link when available.

**Browse** opens the full downloaded calendar, which covers 30 days. Ham2K
manages the download under **Settings → Data Files**. On mobile and desktop,
the last successful download stays available offline.

Calendar data is provided by Joe Leone, W4GGJ. The panel formats and filters
his data for display. [ContestClock](https://contestclock.com/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
