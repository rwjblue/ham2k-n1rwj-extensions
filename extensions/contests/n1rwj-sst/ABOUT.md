The [K1USN Slow Speed Test](https://www.k1usn.com/sst_rules.html) is a good
place to get comfortable on CW. Add **SST** to an operation, pick the session,
and set the name and location you’ll send.

Ham2K can suggest names and locations from past SST contacts and N1MM call
history. It keeps score as you log and exports the session as ADIF or Cabrillo.
