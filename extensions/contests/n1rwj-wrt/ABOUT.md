To log the [Weekly RTTY Test](https://radiosport.world/wrt.html) in Ham2K,
add **WRT** to an operation and choose your session. Set the name and location
you’ll send, then select **RTTY** as your logging mode.

Ham2K can suggest the other operator’s name and location from earlier WRT
contacts. Use **Exports** to save an ADIF or Cabrillo log for the session.
