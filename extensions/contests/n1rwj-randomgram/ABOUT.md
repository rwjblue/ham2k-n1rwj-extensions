In [RandomGram](https://groups.io/g/RandomGram), you send and copy
five-character groups in CW. Every character matters, right down to a zero
at the start of a group.

Add **RandomGram** to a new operation and paste in the list the organizer sent
you. Put the **RandomGram Exchange** panel beside your log so you can see
the exact group you’re about to send as you enter the one you copied.

You can also take part by listening. Leave the transmit list blank and enter
`RGSWL` followed by the group you heard. When you’re done, `RGREPORT` opens
a copyable list of received groups to send to the organizer.
