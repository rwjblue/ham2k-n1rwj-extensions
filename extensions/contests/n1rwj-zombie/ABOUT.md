The [Zombie Shuffle](https://www.zianet.com/qrp/ZOMBIE/pg.html) is a Halloween
CW event where everyone gets a spooky name. This extension covers the
October 30, 2026 event.

Add **Zombie Shuffle 2026** to an operation and set your exchange and UTC
offset for the event date. Ham2K can suggest exchanges from past Zombie
contacts and keeps score as you log.

The optional **Zombie Shuffle** panel has a graveyard that reacts to your
contacts. Its rebirth watch shows when you can work a station again on the
same band. Use the summary export to prepare your submission, or save your
log as ADIF.
